Newsroom charter Why this exists

Less feed.
More understanding.

Ephemerent News covers research, technology, and institutional developments selectively and in depth. It is a place for the explanation that should exist between a technical paper and a confident headline.

  1. 01
    Start with the real question.

    We explain what a result changes, what it does not, and why a reader should care before introducing the machinery.

  2. 02
    Let negative results alter the story.

    A method that disappoints on a harder test is not edited out. That reversal may be the most informative part.

  3. 03
    Keep the record one click away.

    Every research dispatch links back to the manuscript, code, review trail, and underlying artifacts.
