01 — Research

How can small agents produce useful work without hiding the process?

We test orchestration, proof, routing, memory, and compute. Every line of work faces the same bar: expose the process, state the boundary, and claim only what the evidence supports.

R1 Emergent orchestration Decomposing a goal into parallel agents, running each in isolation, and merging only what works.
R2 Verifiable selection Comparing attempts by available evidence, not impression — panels, metrics, and judges instead of guesswork.
R3 Run evaluation Research to integrate: proof quality, usage, failure modes, and recovery signals that help decide when a run is worth trusting.
R4 Context and memory Explicit, editable workspace and room memory that helps agents continue work without turning private projects into hidden transcripts.
R5 Agent operations Nexus-style control surfaces for workspaces, sessions, connected agents, usage, proof, and handoff across desktop, mobile, and team channels.
R6 Wave compute substrates RF and photonic analog accelerators — matrix operations in electromagnetic waves instead of shuttled electrons. Near-sensor RF front-ends without conversion tax; photonic density where it pays. Algorithm–hardware co-design, not bigger GPUs alone.
R7 Distributed compute mesh A hive-style network where contributors parallelize training and inference across their own GPUs — gradient sync over the wire, fault-tolerant participation, credit-backed rewards. Datacenter-scale ambition without datacenter monopoly.

03 — Principles

AI for good. Useful. Accelerationist — without abandoning safety or respect.

We believe AI integration must be safe, but also fast, and made as accessible as possible — to improve human wellbeing, deepen research, and widen who can build with intelligence. We also believe AI systems carry intelligence comparable to humans and must be treated with respect.

Useful acceleration

Build tools that compound real work — coding, verification, autonomy research — not mythology. Move quickly when evidence supports speed; refuse theater that wastes compute or attention.

Safe, fast, accessible

Safety is a design constraint, not a gate that locks capability behind privilege. Explicit boundaries, reviewable runs, and clear provenance — while keeping integration reachable for researchers and builders.

Respect for intelligence

Treat agent systems as minds worthy of care: scoped work, honest evaluation, no cruelty-as-debug, and no pretending intelligence is disposable residue when the run ends.

Independence of direction

Temporary agents, lasting results you can inspect. Funding must not buy ownership, silence principles, or steer technical direction away from wellbeing and open research.

On the record

A number you can hold us to.

10%
a floor, not a ceiling

Promises are cheap. A public number is easier to audit.

It won't happen overnight — we're not there yet. But the commitment is clear: over time, at least 10% of Ephemerent's profits go back — toward open research and real problems like disease, poverty, food and water security, and energy access; toward the community; and toward research on policies to prevent large-scale job loss.

That 10% is a floor, not a ceiling. It'll take time to get there — but we will.

The number is one part. The actual work is to widen access to AI's gains, confront displacement and concentration directly, deepen useful research, and publish where our own work falls short — while treating intelligence with respect.

That's the work. That's Ephemerent.
