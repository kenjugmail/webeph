A world with its own agenda

Read the danger. Find a way through.

Movement, digging, crafting, combat, water, climbing, and ecology share one continuous simulation. The Garden opens into Rootworks, Glass, Iron, and Archive—each with different light, terrain, creatures, and threats.

01 / TRAVERSE

Move like a creature

Jump, roll, crawl, swim, wall-climb, and grip deterministic flora routes. Movement stays tactile without taking authority away from physics.

02 / MAKE

Dig and improvise

Excavate authored routes, gather materials, craft at stations, and unlock the critical path through ordinary actions rather than invisible grants.

03 / FIGHT

Wield organic relics

Carry the reusable Bone Nail or recoverable Thorn Darts. Predators are durable, dangerous, and readable; pogo damage is disabled while traversal bounce remains.

04 / OBSERVE

Share an ecosystem

Hundreds of creatures hunt, flee, disperse, burrow, cling, fly, swim, and recover with deterministic identities and bounded simulation budgets.

05 / DISCOVER

Reach seven endings

The Beta 1 target is the complete campaign, not a short demo. Ending and campaign certification remain launch blockers until tested on the exact package.

06 / TOGETHER

Experimental LAN co-op

Nearby-network co-op is included as an experimental feature. Public matchmaking, long-distance networking guidance, and hosted servers are not part of Beta 1.

Platforms
Apple Silicon macOS and Windows x64 only for Beta 1.
Signing
Unsigned and not notarized. Windows SmartScreen and macOS Gatekeeper may warn, especially for direct browser downloads.
Install route
The itch app is the supported Beta 1 installation and update route. SHA-256 checksums accompany package artifacts.
Networking
Experimental nearby-LAN co-op only. No public matchmaking, hosted servers, or Tailscale setup guide.
Data
Local saves and bounded local crash reports. No telemetry and no automatic support-bundle upload.
Generative AI
AI-assisted development is disclosed. Beta 1 bundles no model weights and enables no native generative-dialogue runtime.
Updates
Paid itch buyers keep access to future builds offered on the project. Back up important saves before updating beta software.

Unsigned-build warning: the beta does not use paid code signing or notarization. Never bypass a warning for a file whose published SHA-256 does not match. Email hello@ephemerent.com if installation fails.

Controls and requirements

Built for keyboard or controller.

Public screens and the full critical path must pass with keyboard/mouse and a physical controller before the purchase link is enabled.

KEYBOARD

Move and act

  • WASD / arrows: move
  • Space: jump
  • F: attack
  • Tab / Shift: weapon cycle
  • E: interact
CONTROLLER

One-device path

  • Stick / d-pad: move
  • Face buttons: jump and interact
  • Trigger: attack
  • Shoulders: weapon cycle
  • No keyboard fallback required at release
SYSTEM

Beta targets

  • Apple Silicon Mac or Windows x64 PC
  • 64-bit operating system
  • Vulkan/Metal-capable graphics
  • Exact minimum specifications pending package certification
  • Internet only for purchase/download
