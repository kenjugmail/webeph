THE ECONOMICS / EDITABLE ASSUMPTIONS

Sentinel may reduce decision-related API spend when it can replace a larger-model call without increasing mistakes. Explore that possibility below. These are illustrative assumptions, not measured customer savings or a price quote.

Calculation: N × Sentinel price + N × fallback fraction × baseline price + N × additional repair fraction × repair price + fixed overhead.

Only decision-call costs are compared. Code generation, other agent work, infrastructure, integration and human review must be included in your own baseline or overhead. No matched end-to-end savings study has been completed. Quality and recovery costs must be measured alongside spend.

Sub-second decisions in local testing. A 12-request deployment smoke measured a 0.73-second median round trip on an RTX 5060 Ti. Public-network latency can exceed one second; longer gameplay prompts also took longer. This is not a throughput or latency guarantee.
