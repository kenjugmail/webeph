Inspection, not certification

Receipts record what was run and what was measured. They do not certify a result, a product or a process, and outputs require qualified human review.

01 · Coding with receipts Available now

Every task ends with a receipt, not a promise.

Orrery proposes each change as a diff you approve, runs your tests, and records the result. The receipt shows what changed, which tests passed, preview screenshots, the model used and what it cost.

  • Tests, preview, cost, model on every run
  • Client budgets: a hard spend cap per client or project
  • AI-use log: one log per client per month, for contracts that ask for AI disclosure
  • Tutor mode: the agent explains and checks your work instead of writing it for you

Budgets, receipts and the AI-use log for client work →

02 · Vision checks on previews In development

A local vision model looks at the preview, so you do not have to squint.

Orrery already captures preview screenshots on coding runs. We are building checks that hand those screenshots to a vision model running on your machine and record what it found: overlapping text, a missing element, a layout that breaks at phone width.

Findings go on the receipt next to the tests. They are a second pair of eyes, not a pass mark: a person still decides.

In development. Not in a released build yet, and the mock on the right is a design sketch, not a screenshot.

03 · Reproducible analysis Early

Results pinned to the data, code, environment and seed that made them.

An analysis is only as good as your ability to run it again. Each notebook cell records hashes of its input data, its code, its environment and its random seed. A rerun check executes the cell again and compares outputs, and the whole chain exports as a receipt you can hand to a reviewer.

Early. Orrery's research notebook exists today; provenance receipts, the rerun check and export are in development.

04 · Engineering inspection with Vellum Engineering Early access

Inspection checks the agent cannot grade for itself.

Vellum Engineering runs manufacturing inspect and audit checks against your parts and assemblies, on your machine. Orrery calls those checks, then re-runs and verifies them itself before anything lands on the receipt, so an agent cannot report a pass it did not earn. From there it drafts a first-article inspection for a qualified inspector to review.

Early access. The Orrery side of the integration is built. AS9102 form export is planned, not shipped. About Vellum →

05 · Sensitive data: local models only Local models available

For data that should never leave the room.

Medical and clinical research data, controlled engineering data: some work cannot go to a hosted model. Point Orrery at models you run yourself (Ollama, LM Studio, vLLM) and keep hosted providers switched off.

With local models, the model runs entirely on your machine: your prompts, code and files are not sent to any model provider. Supports research workflows; outputs require qualified human review.

Sign-in, update checks and opt-in crash reports still contact Ephemerent; they don't include your prompts, code or files.

Orrery is a research and engineering tool. It is not a medical device and does not make a regulated process compliant. Check your own obligations before using it with protected data.
