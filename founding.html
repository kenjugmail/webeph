How it works

Three steps.

  1. Create a free account. Sign up with Google, GitHub or email and confirm it. A seat is tied to a real account, so the 100 go to people who will use them.
  2. Request your seat. Come back here signed in and send the request. I turn on Pro for your account myself, usually the same day, and send the founders channel invite and a link to book your session.
  3. Give it real work. After 30 days your account goes back to free, which still runs the full agent on models you host. Subscribe only if Pro earned it.
Questions

Before you sign up.

Why offer a founding month?
A month gives you time to make Orrery part of your workflow. Direct feedback helps me find out what actually needs fixing and helps you get a setup that fits your repo.
Do I need a card?
No. The founding month is granted to your account directly, with no checkout and no automatic renewal. If you want to keep Pro afterwards, you subscribe like anyone else.
I already subscribe to Pro.
Email me and I'll add you to the founders channel. The feedback line is yours anyway; your billing stays as it is, and you don't use up one of the 100 seats.
What happens to my code?
The same as any Pro run: see Privacy. I only see what you choose to share with me.
What happens at 100?
It closes. The count above is live, and when it reaches zero there are no more founding seats. You can still run your own models free or subscribe to Pro for $40/month, cancel anytime.

Founding 100 is complimentary Pro access for 30 days on one confirmed account per person, limited to 100 seats and granted at Ephemerent's discretion. It does not renew and needs no payment method; normal Pro monthly limits apply, and access may be revoked for abuse. Feedback and sessions are offered on a best-effort basis and are not a service-level agreement. See Terms and Privacy.
