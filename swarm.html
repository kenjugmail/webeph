02 / A COMMUNITY RESOURCE

Many machines.
A common purpose.

Colony connects requests to available contributor capacity. When the network is full or offline, requests stop—not switch to a paid service.

  1. 01

    Contributors opt in

    People choose to offer GPU time. They control their machines and when they participate.

  2. 02

    Capacity becomes available

    The coordinator reports connected workers and models. The playground only enables requests when capacity is reported.

  3. 03

    You send a single prompt

    A volunteer-backed model returns a short response. Capacity can change mid-request; there is no uptime guarantee.
