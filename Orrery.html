Research

Answers you can check, not just read.

Ask a real question. Orrery searches the scholarly indexes for your field, keeps only claims it can quote from a source it found, and tells you plainly what the evidence does not settle.

01

Every field, 14 free indexes

Pick a field (medicine, physics, mathematics, education, law, the humanities and more) and Orrery searches the right sources: PubMed/Europe PMC, ClinicalTrials.gov, arXiv, INSPIRE-HEP, zbMATH, ERIC, DOAJ, HAL, OSF preprints, DataCite, OpenAlex, Crossref and others.

02

Quoted, checked claims

Deep research extracts findings with a verbatim quote, confirms the paper exists, and judges whether the quote supports the claim. The written report cites only verified claims.

03

An evidence gate

Each report shows whether its evidence passes: verified claims, contradictions, and the checks your field needs, such as trial registration and risk of bias for medicine.

04

A budget you set

Every run has a spending cap and a receipt. A typical deep run costs a few cents of its $0.50 default cap.

05

Connected to your work

Send any paper or report to the chat in one click, or ask the chat to run the research itself. Answers link back to the run, and findings save to your Notebook.

06

Honest about gaps

When the literature does not answer the question, the report says so instead of filling the gap. A run the model could not finish is marked as failed, not as done.

Safety boundaries

Automation with a review boundary.

Orrery can make mistakes, and generated code may be insecure, incomplete, or unsuitable for your project. The product is designed to keep work reviewable: scope checks, permission gates, model-route labels, proof summaries, and usage receipts stay visible where supported.

01Review before shippingYou are responsible for accepting, running, and deploying generated code.
02No guaranteed outcomePerformance depends on task, context, models, tools, and provider availability.
03Keep backupsUse source control and inspect changes before relying on them.
04Cloud variesHosted routes, quotas, latency, and providers may change or pause.
Data and account boundaries

Clear about what crosses the network.

Hosted routes send the prompt and code context needed for a completion through Orrery Cloud to the configured provider, and Ephemerent keeps no relay content beyond fulfilling the request. Your Arbiter sessions help make the next Arbiter better at real coding work: anonymously, never tied to your account, and never sold. Prefer not to share? Zero data retention is one switch in settings.

01

Explicit hosted route

Nexus labels the selected Doubleword or Arbiter route before a run starts.

02

Purpose-limited relay

Only the prompt and context needed for the completion are relayed to the configured provider.

03

Content is not metering

Stored usage counters are token totals and timestamps, not copies of prompt content.

04

Your review remains final

Keep source-control backups and inspect changes before accepting, running, or shipping output.

Questions

Before you download.

What leaves my machine?
When you run a hosted route, the prompt and code context needed for that completion pass through Orrery Cloud to the configured provider. Usage counters store totals and timestamps, and Ephemerent keeps no relay content beyond fulfilling the request. See the privacy policy for the full boundary.
Does Orrery learn from my code?
Arbiter does, by default, so each version gets better at the work people actually do: its coding and research sessions (the prompt, the response, and whether the tests passed) are used to train and evaluate the next Arbiter. They are tied to an anonymous session id, never to your account or email, and are never sold or shared. Doubleword requests are never used. Turn on Zero data retention for Arbiter in settings and it keeps only counts from the next request on.
Is there a free trial?
Orrery itself is free with models on your own hardware. Pro is free for 5 days, with its hosted credits, agent runs, and deep research. Checkout asks for a card but charges $0 today; cancel before the trial ends and you are not charged. After 5 days it becomes $40/month.
Which platforms are supported?
Windows (x64), Linux (ARM64) and macOS on Apple silicon (preview); Linux x64 is next. Linux installs update themselves; on Windows and Mac, download the new version from this site. The Mac preview is not notarized yet: open it once, then choose Open Anyway in System Settings, Privacy & Security. The Windows build is not code-signed yet, so Windows may show “Windows protected your PC” the first time: choose More info, then Run anyway.
Can I use Orrery without subscribing?
Yes. With a model server you run yourself (Ollama, LM Studio, vLLM or llama.cpp, on this computer or your local network) the agent is free: pick Your own server in the model menu, no subscription and no API key. Hosted models, deep research on hosted credits, and other providers come with Pro.
Which hosted routes are included?
Pro, Max, and Ultra include monthly Arbiter 27B and Doubleword credits at the amounts shown above. Routes and capacity remain subject to provider availability.
How is the winning attempt chosen?
By execution, not impression. Each attempt is run against your tests, type-checks, and lint; an automatic reviewer scores them and keeps the winner before merging it back.
Can I cancel anytime?
Yes. Paid tiers are monthly via Stripe and cancel anytime — access runs to the end of the paid period. Token quotas reset each billing month and don't roll over.
