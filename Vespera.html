02 — Modes

Choose the shape before you make it.

An evidence-led explainer is only one kind of work. Start by choosing the feeling, pace, and promise the piece needs to keep.

M1 Scientific explainer Linked diagrams, equations, and measured claims with sources beside the cut.
M2 Advertisement Short-film craft that earns a brand ask. Dual 9:16 and 16:9 masters when needed.
M3 Short film Motivated scenes, continuity, sound-led reveals, and an arc.
M4 Visual poem Typography, negative space, texture, and deliberate silence.
M5 Music video Beat anchors, recurring motifs, and compositing timed to the track.
M6 Interactive installation State machines, spatial audio, and responsive scenes.
M7 Comedy lab Timing, contrast, misdirection, and controlled absurdity.
03 — Pipeline

A cut is made in passes.

The browser owns the edit and the encode. Each pass stays visible, so the finished piece still remembers how it got there.

01 Brief Direction, audience, and the shape of the ask named before the first frame.
02 Research & claims Source notes for papers and licensed transcripts. Facts stay close to the moment they shape.
03 Script A multi-beat structure with purpose, visual direction, runtime, and a pace you can feel.
04 Narration Own-voice takes, uploads, or narration profiles — reviewed before they settle into the timeline.
05 Scene Beat-linked scene direction, cue density, and composed motion under a considered camera.
06 Edit Split, trim, move, ripple, mute, lock, and undo — with a playhead that stays in the conversation.
07 Render Browser 1080p30 through WebCodecs and MediaBunny, with a reviewable master and its trail intact.
04 — Evidence

Keep the reason beside the image.

When a piece makes a claim, the source is still there to meet it. Facts, open questions, and interpretation do not blur together.

Verified

Facts backed by source spans, transcripts, or licensed notes.

Unsourced

Statements that still need a citation — visible before they become narration.

Analysis

Interpretation, labeled as such and kept apart from measured claims.

05 — Masters

Leave the work somewhere you can return to.

Cut in the browser

The visual runtime and MediaBunny path produce a master you can re-encode and inspect. Timing and mix decisions stay deliberate.

Plates stay honest

Generative vision plates are reviewed inputs when used. They add material to the cut without pretending to be the cut.
