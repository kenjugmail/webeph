01 — Modes

One studio. Several modes.

An evidence-led explainer is one mode among others. Projects choose a direction before generation begins: a specific image, a clean rhythm, one honest surprise.

M1 Scientific explainer Linked diagrams, equations, and measured claims with sources beside the cut.
M2 Advertisement Short-film craft that earns a brand ask. Dual 9:16 and 16:9 masters when needed.
M3 Short film Motivated scenes, continuity, sound-led reveals, and an arc.
M4 Visual poem Typography, negative space, texture, and deliberate silence.
M5 Music video Beat anchors, recurring motifs, and compositing timed to the track.
M6 Interactive installation State machines, spatial audio, and responsive scenes.
M7 Comedy lab Timing, contrast, misdirection, and controlled absurdity.
02 — Pipeline

From brief to master, in the open.

The browser owns edit and encode. Every stage stays reviewable — not collapsed into one opaque generation.

01 Brief Direction, audience, and constraints named before generation begins.
02 Research & claims Source notes for papers and licensed transcripts. Claims enter a ledger, not a freehand script.
03 Script Multi-beat structure with purpose, visual direction, runtime, and words-per-minute pacing.
04 Narration Own-voice takes, uploads, or selectable narration profiles — with review before they lock to the timeline.
05 Scene Beat-linked Three.js direction, cue density, and deterministic easing under a locked camera.
06 Edit Command-based NLE primitives — split, trim, move, ripple, mute, lock, undo — with a linked playhead.
07 Render Browser 1080p30 through WebCodecs and MediaBunny, with checksums and resumable upload when configured.
03 — Evidence

Claims stay labeled.

Verified facts, unsourced statements, and analysis stay distinct. Unsupported or stale claims can block a render.

Verified

Facts backed by source spans, transcripts, or licensed notes.

Unsourced

Statements that still need a citation — visible before they become narration.

Analysis

Interpretation, labeled as such and kept apart from measured claims.

04 — Masters

Masters lock. Plates are optional.

Locked in the browser

The visual runtime and MediaBunny path produce a master you can re-encode and inspect. Timing and mix decisions stay intentional.

Plates stay optional

Generative vision plates are reviewed inputs when used. They do not replace the locked master.
